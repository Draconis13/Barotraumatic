52cf90bafb: Attempt at making hammerheads less frustrating and less "seafloor gameplay", first iteration (experimental and wip)
Regular hammerheads now also avoid gunfire with a damage threshold of 20 (regular coilgun ammo won't do)
Golden hammerheads also require a damage threshold of 20 (regular coilgun ammo won't do anymore)
Less structure damage for both hammerheads, making instant flooding of the whole submarine a bit less likely
Use CreateWallDamageProjectiles="true"  and <SpawnItem identifier="walldamageprojectile"> to spawn projectiles even when only very little walldamage is done
Made golden hammerheads actually rare (10% chance instead of 25% chance) in Europa Ridge


f0a763ebb6: Fix to threshers being able to keep you stunlocked indefinitely. It seems this was caused by cd57144, which gave the threshers an attack that makes them start eating you after the initial bite. It only has a 2 second cooldown, so in practice they can keep eating you indefinitely. Another issue was that monsters eating someone would not let go when switching away from the attack state except when they switch to another attack limb.
